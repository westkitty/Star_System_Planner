THE CARTOGRAPHER'S DESK

This is a spatial hypothesis, not a canonical star chart. Every starting point and corridor is editable draft geometry. Only verified names and relationships should be promoted through separate canon governance.

EXPLORATION

Drag or swipe to orbit. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Select a marker. Double-click a galaxy to explore its systems; double-click a system to inspect planets.

AUTHORING

Use the inspector to rename and reposition draft objects, change their typography, add annotations, or connect draft routes. Edits are saved in this browser and can be exported as JSON.

KEYBOARD

H: hide HUD. /: search. Escape: up one scale. F: focus selection. Home: return to the All.

CANON BOUNDARY

WorldsVault identities and documented relationships have references. None of the map's coordinates, proposed regions, displayed routes, or exemplary planetary orbits is established lore.
