## Established history

The Blood Eclipse War lasted **170 years**. The following are *war-relative markers*, not absolute dates:

| Marker | Event |
| --- | --- |
| Year 0 | Fringe-colony hostilities; Drakken lightning-strike terraforming triggers Administration counter-macros. |
| Year 3 | First Blood Rings erected at Fallenstar Prime. |
| Years 7–120 | Long attrition; countless worlds lost and only short-lived Administration victories. |
| Year 121 | Siege of the Ruby Eclipse, an Administration assault in the Pharos Nebula. |
| Year 170 | Aureal Gate collapse and the first Drakken weaponization of Starsilk against divine authority. |

The terminal **causal order**, with no invented intermediary dates, is:

1. Year 170: Drakken use Starsilk against divine authority at Aureal Gate.
2. Tiger responds with the Hal’Ven heliocide and sequential star collapse.
3. The resulting singularities form the nascent Siege Wall lattice.
4. The permanent Siege Wall is locked in the post-war aftermath.

The Hushing of Shail-9 is among the documented **wartime** incidents, but its exact war year is unspecified. The First Dirt occurs **31 days post-war**.

## Rejected/unsupported interpretations

A separately compressed administrative duration is **not independently canonized**. The older Google Drive ledger once treated a supposed administrative timescale as a competing official chronology. That claim is unsupported by the controlling Dossier, and may not override its authored duration or dates. Historical source quotations must be marked deprecated rather than promoted to canon.

## Unknowns and Atlas boundaries

Absolute calendar dates, exact heliocide/locking timestamps, historical galaxy coordinates, historical world positions, route distances, and planetary census for any selected era are **not authored**. The three Atlas snapshots (`war`, `locking`, `post`) are **independent editable editorial maps**, not a 170-frame reconstruction. The app must neither migrate user-created snapshots nor rewrite coordinate data to fit these event markers.
