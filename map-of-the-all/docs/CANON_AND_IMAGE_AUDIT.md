### Mechanical locks

- Starsilk is luminous cyan/azure programmable substrate; it is not animated life and the flow's curl is a visual encoding only.
- The Siege Wall follows Tiger's heliocide and is **a black-hole lattice / starless absence** containing the Drakken, not a geological stone cliff.
- Blood Rings are vitrified remnants of destroyed worlds, not benign ringworld megastructures; red is a restricted accent rather than the palette.
- The Blood Eclipse War lasted 170 years (machine invariant); views are qualitative (`war`, `locking`, `post`) and do not claim a chronology of all shown unverified galaxies.
- Do not invent Starsilk-affecting deaths, save-the-day outcomes or political cartography. No direct character art appears, so no canonical character image reference handoff is implicated.

## Source-truth types in prototype

- `status:'reference'`: source identity/link only; node editing forbidden; display position still `provisional`.
- `status:'draft'`: wholly editorial structure; freely editable; never changes external source.
- `sourceUrl`: only allow approved origin `https://westkitty.github.io/Starsilk_Character_Dossier/` in original records. Import deliberately sets all imported statuses to `draft` and removes source references.
